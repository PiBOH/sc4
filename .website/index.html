SimCity 4 locale archives

Updated SimCityLocale.dat files for SimCity 4 Deluxe v1.1.610 (compatible up to v1.1.641). Choose a language, download the ZIP, replace the original archive, and play in your preferred locale.

13 language archives: Danish, Dutch, English, Finnish, French, German, Italian, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, and UK English.

Browse languages Releases

How it works

  • Each language folder contains a validated SimCityLocale.dat / .DAT DBPF archive.
  • Releases include per-language ZIPs and a full source package with SHA-256 checksums.
  • Archives are parsed, rebuilt, and validated before publication.
  • The repository is public domain (Unlicense). SimCity 4 remains the property of its rights holders.

Compatibility

Tested on SimCity 4 Deluxe v1.1.610, compatible up to v1.1.641.
